# DEMO-MNT-01 — Synthetic maintenance procedure

This file is synthetic demonstration material, not an operating instruction or industry standard.

1. Record observed vibration, bearing temperature and seal condition with units.
2. Compare observations to the equipment-specific OEM limits. If the OEM limits are unavailable, record "OEM limits required; compliance not determined".
3. Escalate abnormal leakage to the responsible maintenance engineer for review.
4. Do not recommend defeating an interlock, relief device or protective shutdown.
5. An approval note must distinguish observations, assumptions, calculations and proposed actions.
6. All generated engineering conclusions require qualified human review before operational use.
